# DotStark AI Booking Agent

**Client overview**

## What it is

DotStark AI Booking Agent lets your customers book appointments by chatting. A customer opens a chat window on your website, or messages you on WhatsApp, and an AI assistant guides them to a confirmed booking. No forms to fill. No phone tag. You manage everything from one dashboard: your hours, the questions the assistant asks, and every conversation and booking.

## Who it's for

Any team that runs appointments: clinics and practices, salons and spas, law and accounting firms, tutors and coaches, agencies, and service businesses. If your team spends time answering "do you have availability?" questions, this product handles that work around the clock.

## How it works

### For your customers

A customer opens the chat and types what they want, for example "I need a consultation next Tuesday afternoon." The assistant asks for the details you need, shows real available times in the customer's timezone, and confirms the booking. If you require it, the customer verifies their email with a one-time code before the booking is confirmed. The customer receives a confirmation email. They can reschedule or cancel later in the same chat.

### For your team

You set up the assistant once: your business name, the fields you want collected, weekly hours, slot length, and how far ahead customers can book. A calendar shows every booking. An inbox lists every conversation with its channel and status. When a customer needs a human, you take over the conversation from the inbox, chat live, and hand it back to the AI when done. If a customer asks about your services, the assistant answers from documents you upload or your website content.

## Capabilities

| Capability | What it means |
|---|---|
| Conversational booking | Customers book by typing. The assistant collects and checks the details you define, from names to service-specific fields. |
| Live availability | Real-time slots based on your schedule, breaks, and exception days, shown in the customer's timezone. |
| No double-bookings | A slot is reserved while the customer confirms, and the booking is committed safely. Two customers can never take the same time. |
| Email verification | An optional one-time code confirms the customer's email before booking, filtering mistyped or fake contacts. |
| Automatic confirmations | Confirmation emails go to the customer and to your team. |
| Calendar and meeting links | Bookings appear in Google Calendar or Outlook. For online-only businesses, meeting links are created automatically from Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom. |
| Human handoff | Your team takes over a conversation from the inbox, replies live, and releases it back to the AI. |
| Knowledge answers | The assistant answers questions about your business from PDFs you upload and your website pages. |
| Self-service changes | Customers reschedule or cancel in chat, without calling you. |
| WhatsApp channel | The same assistant answers on WhatsApp Business. |
| One dashboard | Setup, bookings, conversations, and integrations in a single place. |
| Data isolation | Each business keeps its own settings, documents, and data. |

## Glossary

- **Chat widget** — the small chat window embedded on your website.
- **OTP** — one-time password: a short code sent by email to confirm the customer's address.
- **Slot** — one bookable time period, for example 30 minutes.
- **Booking window** — how far ahead customers can book.
- **Meeting link** — a video call link from Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom.
- **Knowledge answers** — the assistant reads your documents (PDFs and website pages) to answer questions, and shows the page it drew from.
- **WebSocket** — a connection that delivers new messages instantly instead of checking repeatedly.
- **Multi-tenant** — one system serves many businesses, with each business's data kept separate.
